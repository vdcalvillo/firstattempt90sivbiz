Starting out as an underground concert venue, The Graduate welcomed a variety of grunge and rock musical acts for students to enjoy, including The Cramps as pictured in the ticket above. 
![The Graduate Newspaper](https://vdcalvillo.github.io/firstattempt90sivbiz/iiif/objects/img6355/img6355.jpg)

Eventually, the Graduate would close, with the owner of the venue citing that he was "tired of dealing with kids". However, the venue would go on to still support nightlife happenings in Isla Vista under a different name.
